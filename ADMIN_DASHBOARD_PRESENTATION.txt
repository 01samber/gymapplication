SLIDE 1 — Title
SweatBox Gym Admin Dashboard
Complete Workflow Overview
Your command center for gym operations

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 2 — Overview
What is the Admin Dashboard?
A unified web platform for gym staff to manage members, trainers, bookings, nutrition, and revenue — all in one place.
Access: Web browser (desktop or tablet)
Login: Email and password (admin role required)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 3 — Dashboard Home
Dashboard Overview
The first screen after login shows:

• Total Members — All registered gym members
• Active Trainers — Staff available for sessions
• Today's Bookings — PT sessions scheduled
• Monthly Revenue — Subscription income at a glance
• Today's Check-ins — Members who visited today
• Pending Requests — New registrations awaiting approval
• Recent Activity — Latest bookings and updates
• Quick Actions — One-click shortcuts to Add Member, Create Booking, Manage Trainers, View Reports

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 4 — Add Member Flow (Step 1)
Adding a New Member — Step 1
Click "Add Member" from the dashboard or Members page.

Enter member details:
• Full Name (required)
• Email (required)
• Phone (optional)
• Subscription Plan (required) — choose from 4 plans:
  - Normal Gym — $150/month
  - Personal Training — $350/month
  - Nutrition Plan — $300/month
  - Premium Package — $550/month
• Fitness Goal (optional) — Weight loss, Muscle gain, General fitness, Strength, Endurance

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 5 — Add Member Flow (Step 2)
Adding a New Member — Step 2
After submitting:
• System creates the account instantly
• Admin receives a temporary password to share with the client
• Client must change password on first login in the mobile app
• Member is automatically added to the roster with an active 1-month subscription
• Loyalty tracking starts from day one

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 6 — Registrations (Incoming Requests)
Registration Requests
For members who sign up via the mobile app first:

• View all pending, approved, or rejected requests
• Filter by status (pending, approved, rejected)
• Review each request: name, email, phone, requested plan, fitness goal
• Approve: Adds member and grants access
• Reject: Option to add notes explaining why
• Badge shows pending count in sidebar for quick attention

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 7 — Members Management
Members Page
Central hub for all gym members:

• View all members in card or list format
• Search by name or email
• Filter by fitness goal (weight loss, muscle gain, etc.)
• Edit member details (name, email, phone, goal)
• Delete members when needed
• See join date and subscription status
• Quick stats: Total members, members with active goals

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 8 — Trainers
Trainers Management
Manage your personal training team:

• View all trainers and their profiles
• Add new trainers to the system
• Edit trainer details, specializations, certifications
• Activate or deactivate trainers
• See trainer capacity and availability
• Trainers appear in dropdowns when creating bookings

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 9 — Bookings
Bookings (PT Sessions)
Schedule and manage personal training sessions:

• Calendar view — pick a date to see that day's sessions
• View all bookings: client, trainer, time slot, status
• Create new booking: select client, trainer, date, start/end time
• Update booking status: Pending, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled
• Filter by status or date
• Sessions show client and trainer names for easy identification

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 10 — Attendance
Attendance & Check-ins
Track who visits the gym each day:

• Daily view — see all check-ins for a selected date
• Member stats — monthly visits, total visits, last visit per member
• Search members by name
• View check-in time for each member
• Identify regular attendees vs. those who need follow-up
• Data feeds into loyalty and retention reports

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 11 — Client Nutrition
Client Nutrition (Dietitian Integration)
Admin overview of nutrition programs:

• View clients with diet plans assigned
• See diet plan status: Active, Completed, Paused, Draft
• View body composition history (weight, BMI, body fat, etc.)
• Track meal logs and adherence
• See cheat days and meal commitments
• Navigate between clients to review nutrition progress
• Complements the dedicated Dietitian app for detailed meal planning

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 12 — Exercises
Exercises Library
Manage the exercise database for workouts:

• Add, edit, delete exercises
• Fields: Name, description, muscle group, equipment, difficulty
• Video URL and image URL for demonstrations
• Used by trainers and clients in workout plans
• Filter and search exercises
• Categorize for easy discovery (e.g., chest, legs, cardio)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 13 — Subscriptions
Subscriptions Management
Monitor and manage member plans:

• View all active, expired, or cancelled subscriptions
• See plan type, start date, end date, price, status
• Filter by status or plan type
• Add or extend subscriptions for members
• Track revenue per plan
• Identify renewals and expirations

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 14 — Requests
Trainer & Dietitian Requests
Members can request trainers or dietitians from the app:

• View pending, approved, or rejected requests
• Approve or reject with admin notes
• Assign trainer or dietitian to the member
• Filter by status
• Badge in sidebar for pending items
• Keeps admin informed of member needs

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 15 — Loyalty
Loyalty Program
Reward long-term members:

• View loyalty tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum
• Track consecutive months per member
• See who qualifies for discounts or free sessions
• Monitor total points and streaks
• Encourage retention with visible rewards
• Data from subscription history and attendance

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 16 — Reports
Reports & Analytics
Business and nutrition insights:

Business Reports:
• Total revenue, new members, completed sessions
• Member retention percentage
• Revenue by month (chart)
• New members by month (chart)
• Revenue growth trends

Nutrition Reports:
• Total diet plans, active plans
• Clients with plans vs. without
• Average completion rate
• Meal logs this week
• Popular meal types
• Plan type breakdown (weekly vs. monthly)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 17 — Settings
Settings
Configure the gym system:

• Gym profile and contact info
• Default settings for new members
• Notification preferences
• Integration options
• Admin user management (if supported)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SLIDE 18 — End-to-End Workflow Summary
Typical Day Workflow

Morning:
1. Log in — see dashboard with today's stats
2. Check pending registrations — approve or reject new sign-ups
3. Review today's bookings — confirm sessions

During the day:
4. Add walk-in members via Add Member
5. Create bookings when clients call or visit
6. Monitor attendance as members check in
7. Handle trainer/dietitian requests as they arrive

End of day:
8. Review reports — revenue, retention, nutrition stats
9. Plan next day — check upcoming bookings and expiring subscriptions

SLIDE 19 — Key Benefits
Why This Dashboard?

• Single login for all operations — no switching between tools
• Real-time data — see updates as they happen
• Mobile-friendly — use on tablet at reception
• Secure — role-based access, only admins see this
• Integrated — members, trainers, nutrition, and finance in one place
• Scalable — add more members and plans without limits
